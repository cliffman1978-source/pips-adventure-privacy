Saves and availability

Progress and inventory are saved on your device. In Android builds with cloud saving, the game also connects to Google Play Games and backs up automatically when your profile and the service are available; no separate Oni Labs Games account is required. To recover progress on another Android device, use the same Play Games profile. When different saved progress is found, the game shows the cloud and device level and coin balances. You must choose Use Google Play Save or Keep Current Progress before continuing. Using the Google Play save replaces this device’s progress and keeps a local recovery copy; keeping current progress preserves this device’s state and enables its automatic backups. Device histories and balances are not merged. Backups can be delayed by network, account or service problems, and recovery is not guaranteed. Uninstalling, clearing app data, losing your device or deleting backups may lose progress and consumables. Contact support before resetting data. Apple/iCloud saving and Android-to-iPhone transfer are not included.
