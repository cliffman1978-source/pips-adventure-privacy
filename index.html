Pip’s Adventure Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Oni Labs Games provides Pip’s Adventure for iPhone, iPad and Android. Choose your platform: iPhone and iPad or Android. For help or privacy requests, email onilabsgames@gmail.com.

iPhone and iPad

Play does not require a game account. The game saves progress, settings, coins, lives, inventory and purchase-delivery records on your device. This version has no in-game cloud sync. Deleting the app can remove your local progress and inventory. Device backups are managed separately through your Apple settings.

Purchases

Apple processes optional purchases through the App Store. The game uses StoreKit on your device to verify the purchase and uses the product ID, transaction ID and quantity to deliver the items once. It records delivery locally before marking the transaction finished. Check Purchases retries unfinished transactions. We do not receive your payment-card details. The Google Play verification service described below applies only to Android.

Rewarded ads and privacy choices

Watch & Earn lets you choose to view an ad for an in-game reward. The game stores your selected age range on the device and enables its advertising and consent flow only for players who select 18 or over. Google’s consent service checks what choices apply in your region before the game requests ads. When available, open Watch & Earn and select Ad Privacy Choices to revisit them.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and crash and performance diagnostics. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, operate its services and prevent fraud. The data depends on the SDK configuration, device settings and your privacy choices. Test ads can also contact Google. See Google’s iOS advertising data disclosure and Google’s privacy policy.

Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and any details or screenshots you send. We use Gmail to handle these messages and keep them as long as needed to respond, resolve an issue or meet legal obligations. To request access, correction or deletion of your support information, email onilabsgames@gmail.com. Apple manages App Store transactions under Apple’s privacy policy.

Android

The following sections describe the Android version, including Google Play Games cloud saving in builds that support it, Google Play purchases and rewarded advertising.

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, completed levels and quests, inventory, virtual currency, lives, game settings, and local diagnostic information on your device so that you can continue playing. It may also create a random installation identifier and local records used by the game’s reward system. The game does not require you to create an account or provide your name or email address to play.

Your level progress and inventory are stored locally and, in builds with cloud saving, backed up through Google Play Games when available. We do not operate a separate cloud game-account service. Purchase verification sends the limited information described below to our verification service. Builds with rewarded ads also use Google’s advertising and consent services as described below. The game does not include a separate developer-operated gameplay analytics service.

Automatic Google Play Games cloud saves

In builds with this feature, the game connects to Google Play Games and backs up progress automatically when your Play Games profile and Google’s services are available. There is no separate game login or in-game backup on/off switch. Google may require its own profile setup or account selection. You can view connection and backup information under Menu → Settings → Save Status. Local saving remains available if Google Play Games is unavailable.

To back up and recover your adventure, the game accesses your Play Games player identifier and display name. Backups contain game progress such as levels and stars, buildings, quests, coins, lives, inventory, unfinished bonus rounds, reward and purchase-delivery records, and the random installation identifier stored with your progress. Backup descriptions contain the device model, level and save time. Each device’s backup uses an identifier derived from a SHA-256 hash of Android’s app-scoped device identifier, or a random fallback identifier; our cloud-save code does not transmit the raw Android identifier. These identifiers distinguish profiles and device saves and help prevent duplicate purchase delivery. The cloud-save feature does not request your Google password or email address, read unrelated Drive files, or upload game screenshots.

Google stores and processes the backups through Play Games Saved Games. The game can read and write this game’s saves for the authenticated profile. Play Games also processes gamer identity, service analytics and diagnostics under Google’s policies. Google documents encryption in transit using HTTPS. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Play Games data-handling information.

On another Android device, use the same Play Games profile. The game checks for existing backups and may ask you to choose between Use Google Play Save and Keep Current Progress, showing the level and coins for each. A choice is required before continuing from this prompt. Using the Google Play save replaces local progress; a local recovery copy is kept. Keeping current progress preserves the device’s state and starts its backups. Different devices’ histories and balances are not automatically merged. Offline changes may sync later, and a locally accepted backup does not guarantee it has reached Google’s servers.

Android backup and platform services

Android backup is permitted by the game’s configuration. Depending on your device and Google account settings, Android may back up and restore app data through Google’s backup services. These backups are managed by your device and Google, rather than by a game account operated by us. You can manage backup through your device and Google account settings.

Google Play and your operating system may separately process download, device, security, crash, or diagnostic information under their own settings and policies. We may receive app performance reports through Google Play to identify and fix problems. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Optional coin packs, booster packs, hearts and bonus-game tokens are purchased through Google Play. Google processes payment and billing information under its own policies. We do not receive your payment-card number or banking details.

To verify and deliver a purchase, the game sends the product identifier, Google Play purchase token and a random installation identifier to our HTTPS verification service hosted on Render. The installation identifier is also supplied to Google Play as an obfuscated account identifier; it is not your email address or a game login. Our service checks the purchase with Google and processes purchase status and quantity to prevent duplicate or invalid rewards.

The verification database stores a SHA-256 hash of the purchase token, the installation identifier and the product identifier. The application does not store the raw purchase token in that database. Google and our hosting provider may also process network information such as IP addresses and technical logs to operate and secure their services. See Render’s Privacy Policy.

The game keeps local receipt and reward records so a verified transaction is not delivered twice. “Check Purchases” retries unfinished purchases; it does not restore previously consumed items or provide cloud synchronization of your inventory. Clearing app storage may remove local purchased items and progress.

Optional rewarded ads and privacy choices

Builds with advertising enabled use Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Ads are optional: you choose a reward in Watch & Earn and start an ad yourself. You can continue playing without watching ads. Some rewards require two completed ads; progress toward those rewards is saved on your device.

Before the game requests consent or loads an ad, it asks you to select an age range or prefer not to say. The game saves this choice in its local app storage and remembers it between sessions, so restarting does not ask again. It does not request your date of birth, obtain your age from your phone or Google Play account, or send the selected range to our purchase-verification server. The game does not start its advertising or consent integration for players who select under 18 or prefer not to say. The saved choice is subject to the Android backup and local-data retention practices described in this policy. This age choice is separate from Google's advertising consent choices; remembering it does not bypass the game's consent checks.

For players who select 18 or over, Google’s services may process IP address (including approximate location inferred from it), device and advertising identifiers, ad and app interaction information, and diagnostics. The purposes include delivering and measuring ads, service analytics, security and fraud prevention. Information processed depends on the SDK, device settings, region and privacy choices. Production builds use live rewarded ads. Test builds may use sample ads; testing does not mean no network data is processed.

Where required, Google displays a privacy message before ads are requested. You can revisit available choices through Watch & Earn → Ad Privacy Choices, including withdrawal of consent or opting out of sale or sharing where offered. This button appears when Google indicates a privacy form is available for that purpose. Google stores consent signals on the device and uses them when processing ad requests. An opt-out may still permit limited or non-personalized ads; it is not a guarantee of no data processing. Device settings also provide controls for the Android advertising ID.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not send your email address, purchase token or local game inventory to AdMob through our ad-reward code. Rewards are recorded locally; this version does not use an advertising server-to-server reward-verification service.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to send, such as your name, device details, screenshots, or a description of a problem. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot, and handle privacy requests. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or information that is unnecessary for your request.

Support correspondence is handled through Gmail. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver and store messages. We do not sell your support information or use it for targeted advertising.

Sharing and processing locations

We use Google Play for purchases, Google Play Games for automatic cloud saves and profile connection, Render for purchase verification, Google AdMob and UMP for enabled advertising and consent features, and Gmail for support. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or where necessary to protect rights, prevent abuse, or address security issues. Providers may process information outside your province or country under their own privacy protections and applicable law.

Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until you clear the game’s app storage or remove it, subject to Android backup and restore settings. Clearing storage permanently removes local progress. Uninstalling the game does not necessarily delete a separately stored device backup; manage those copies through your backup provider.

Google Play Games cloud backups are separate from the local save and Android device backup. Uninstalling or clearing app storage does not necessarily delete these cloud copies. The game maintains a backup slot per device and does not apply an automatic expiry to these slots. Google manages retention under its policies. You can delete game data through your Play Games profile; follow Google’s instructions for deleting individual game data. You can also manage the game’s Google account access and Play Games sign-in through Google’s settings. Deleting Google-held game data does not delete local saves, support emails or purchase-verification records. Continuing to play while connected can create a new cloud backup from remaining local progress. If you want to stop future cloud backups, manage or remove the game’s access through Google before playing again. Contact us for help identifying which copies or records you want removed.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to address your request, resolve related issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent us by emailing onilabsgames@gmail.com. We may need to verify that a request comes from the person concerned. Information required for legal obligations or an unresolved dispute may be retained for that purpose.

Purchase-verification records are kept to prevent duplicate fulfilment and investigate purchase problems. The current verification service has no automatic expiry for these records. You may request deletion by contacting us; records needed for fraud prevention, an unresolved transaction or applicable legal obligations may need to be retained. Clearing app storage does not delete server-side verification records. Google, Render and other platform providers manage their own records and retention under their policies. We do not promise that deleting our records deletes their records.

For access, correction, deletion or questions about privacy choices, email the contact below. Describe the relevant purchase or device without sending passwords, full payment-card details or purchase tokens. We may request limited information needed to locate the record and verify the request.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards for information we handle, including limiting access to support correspondence. Purchase verification and Play Games cloud-save transfers use HTTPS. Local game data relies on your device’s security protections. No device, transmission method, or storage service can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children’s privacy

The game’s declared target audience is ages 13 and over. Advertising is available only after a player selects 18 or over; selecting a younger age or skipping the age choice leaves ads off. The game remembers this choice in local app storage between sessions. This does not disable other network features, such as Google Play purchases and available Play Games cloud-saving services. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so that we can review and delete it as appropriate. Parents and guardians can manage device permissions, backup settings, and access to email.
